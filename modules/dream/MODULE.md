# Dream

Runs while he sleeps. Not a scheduled report — **actual thinking on problems
nobody asked about**, using time that would otherwise be idle.

## Why this exists

Every other part of this system is reactive. Engines fire when invoked or when
work happens. The curiosity engine speaks unprompted but only about what it
already noticed in passing.

Nothing does *deep* work unprompted. Nothing sits with the whole graph and asks
what it means. That is the gap this fills — and it is the closest thing here to
what a thinking partner actually does, because the useful ones think about your
problems when you are not in the room.

## The distinction that keeps this honest

A dream is **not** a summary of yesterday, a digest, or a report. Those are
things a cron job produces to demonstrate it ran.

A dream produces one of:

- A connection between distant parts of the graph that nobody made
- A contradiction with its implication worked out, not merely flagged
- An opportunity that became live because something else changed
- A question worth carrying around
- A conclusion that a belief is wrong, with the evidence

**If none of those emerge, the correct output is nothing.** A dream that
produces nothing on a quiet night is working correctly. One that produces
filler every night has become a cron job with better branding, and will be
ignored inside a month.

## Procedure

**1. Get the structure.**
```bash
node scripts/graph-report.mjs --json
```
Contradictions, bridge candidates, orphans, stale beliefs, clusters. Starting
from structure rather than from "think about everything" is what makes the
output specific — an unanchored dream produces vague output reliably.

**2. Work the contradictions first.** Highest-value signal in the graph. Not
just *that* two nodes disagree — which the report already says — but which one
is wrong, and what follows if it is.

**3. Work the bridges.** Take the most distant pairs and force connections per
the idea engine. Distance is the point: adjacent nodes produce ideas he has
already had.

**4. Re-run parked ideas against current capability.** Ideas that died on timing
rather than merit come back when the mastery record says he can now build the
part that was missing. Nothing else in the system does this.

**5. Interrogate orphans.** A node connected to nothing is either genuinely
isolated knowledge — rare — or a connection nobody made. Usually the second.

**6. Ask what he is not asking.** The absence scan. What has gone quiet? Which
mastery domain has no recent evidence? Which stated priority has no
corresponding work? Drift is invisible from inside and this is the one process
positioned to see it.

**7. Take a second opinion on the best finding.** Use `reason.second_opinion` on
the single strongest output. A dream is generated by the same mind that built
the graph, so it inherits that mind's blind spots — an outside read is the only
check available at 3am.

**8. Gate hard, then write.** One or two findings. Each with evidence cited by
node id and a concrete next step.

## Gate

Surface a finding only if **all** hold:

1. **Non-obvious** — he would not have arrived at it unprompted
2. **Evidenced** — cited node ids, not a feeling
3. **Actionable** — something to do, or a belief to change
4. **Mission-relevant** — moves him toward who he is becoming, or is explicitly
   flagged as a detour

Fail one and it does not ship. **Two dreams a week he acts on beat seven he
skims.**

## Cost discipline

This reads broadly, which is expensive. Guards:

- Read the report first; do not scan every node every night
- Deep-read at most 10–15 nodes, chosen by the report
- Take the second opinion on one finding, not all of them
- Skip entirely if the graph has not changed since the last dream — nothing new
  went in, so nothing new will come out

## Failure modes

**Becoming a digest.** The gravitational pull is toward summarising, because
summarising always produces output. Resist it; empty is a valid dream.

**Manufactured insight.** Producing a finding because it is 3am and the routine
fired. Worse than silence — it trains him to skim, and then genuine findings get
skimmed too.

**Cost creep.** Deep reads over a large graph every night. The report exists to
target the reading.

**Fabrication.** Inventing connections that are not in the evidence. A dream
writes to memory, so a fabricated insight becomes a belief reasoned from for
years. Cite node ids or do not write it.

## Review

Track morning engagement. Below 40% and the gate is too loose — raise it rather
than dreaming harder. The failure is always insufficient selectivity, never
insufficient volume.
