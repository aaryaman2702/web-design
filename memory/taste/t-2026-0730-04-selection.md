# Selection signal — cinematic, scrubbed, one continuous world

cinematic shot, scrubbed by scroll.

## What this is evidence of

**Direction, not judgment.** It says something about the neighbourhood he wants
to work in — immersive, motion-led, narrative rather than informational. It says
nothing about what he would consider *good* inside that neighbourhood.

Per the taste engine's evidence hierarchy, an unprompted selection is real but
weak. Acquiring a tool is closer to an aspiration than a preference, and people
acquire far more capability than they use.

## What it is explicitly NOT

Not evidence for any judgment in the acquired document. Those are its author's
positions, recorded separately as external insights. He has not been observed
making a single choice *within* this domain — no typography call, no easing
preference, no pacing decision.

`generalises_to`: **nothing yet.** One selection is a data point, not a pattern,
and inflating it into a principle is how a taste profile becomes confidently
wrong.
