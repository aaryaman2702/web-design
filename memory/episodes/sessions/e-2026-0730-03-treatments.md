# Built three treatments of one scroll-film concept

Concept "Descent" — a deep-water exploration homepage. Three incompatible
answers to one brief, each live and scroll-driven, built to elicit taste
judgments rather than to ship.

Prediction sealed in git at `ae88ca6` before building. Result pending.

## Worked

**Forced divergence produced the two interesting options.** The first instinct
for "scroll-film about a descent" is the cinematic one — that idea arrived
immediately and would have been the whole deliverable under time pressure. The
instrument treatment and the logbook treatment only appeared after deliberately
asking what a different discipline would do with the same brief.

Confirms the divergence movement in `core/cognition.md` rather than discovering
it, but confirmation from practice is worth more than the doctrine alone.

**Pure-code motion was sufficient.** Three convincing treatments, zero credits
spent, no footage. Worth knowing before committing budget to generated video:
the *direction* can be tested cheaply and only the winner needs real footage.

## Didn't work

**A syntax error shipped into the file.** A stray bracket in a CSS declaration
(`border-color:#123]`) survived writing and re-reading. Caught by a mechanical
check afterwards, not by eye.

## Surprised

How much the artifact changed when each treatment was made to state **what it
costs you**. Before that, three options read as a menu with an obvious winner.
After, they read as three genuine positions with real trade-offs — and the
question became answerable.
