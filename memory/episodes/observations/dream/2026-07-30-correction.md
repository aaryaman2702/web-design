# Correction — dream finding 2 rested on a fabricated premise

Episodes are immutable, so the original dream stands unedited. This supersedes
its second finding.

## What was claimed

Finding 2 of `o-dream-2026-0730` stated that
`.claude/skills/scroll-film-studio/` was *"his own authored skill"* and *"a
document he wrote about how he thinks web experiences should look and move"* —
and recommended ingesting it into the taste engine as his aesthetic evidence.

## What is true

He did not write it.

- Byte-identical to the harness-distributed copy — `diff` returns nothing
- Entered via "Add files via upload" from *"Fable 5 Skill (resource).zip"*
- Written as second-person instructions to a model

## How it happened

The dream observed a craft-heavy document sitting in his repository next to an
empty taste profile, and filled the gap between *"this is here"* and *"this is
his"* with an assumption that was never checked. The assumption then acquired
the confident register of a finding.

Nothing in the dream's own evidence supported authorship. Provenance was simply
never asked about.

## Why this is the expensive kind of error

Ingesting it would have written a stranger's aesthetic into
`memory/taste/profile.md` as Aaryaman's. Taste is the one instrument whose
entire value is that it models *him* — corrupting it does not produce obvious
errors, it produces confident, plausible, subtly-wrong design proposals for
years, with no signal that anything is wrong.

Constitution VI: *a fabricated fact recorded today is a false belief reasoned
from for years.* This is precisely that shape.

## What caught it

The ingest module's attribution rule — external context is testimony, not
observation — which forced a provenance check before writing. The check took one
`diff`.

**The dream did not catch its own error.** It was caught at the next stage, by a
module with a different job. That is an argument for keeping generation and
ingestion as separate steps with separate standards, rather than letting a
process that generates findings also write them to memory.

## What survives from finding 2

The underlying observation was correct and remains unaddressed: **craft
dimensions in the taste profile are all zero while craft is the first mastery
domain.** The instrument for his most important domain has no readings.

What was wrong was the proposed shortcut. There is no cache of his taste sitting
unread on disk. It has to be generated by him making and explaining choices,
which is slower and cannot be skipped.

## Lesson

Before treating anything in the repository as evidence about him, establish
whether he made it. Presence is not authorship. The check is cheap; the error is
not.
