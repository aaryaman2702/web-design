# Business

Reading a business the way an engineer reads a stack trace: where the leverage
is, where the leak is, what one change moves the most.

**Weakest domain, largest headroom.** Craft plus engineering without business
produces an excellent contractor. The combination is the actual moat, and this
is the missing third.

**Next concrete step:** one complete loop — positioning through pricing through
delivery through retrospective — with the reasoning written down. One documented
loop teaches more than a year of unexamined delivery, because only the recorded
version can be reasoned about later.
