# Judgment

The compounding domain. Decision quality under uncertainty, calibration, taste
in problem selection, the ability to hold a strong opinion and still update.

Compounds hardest because it improves every other domain. A better decision
about what to build beats a better execution of the wrong thing, and no amount
of craft recovers from choosing badly.

**Instrument:** the decision engine. Unmeasurable until roughly ten decisions
carry written expectations — and unfakeable after that, which is the point.
