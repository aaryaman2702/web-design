# Craft

Work that makes people feel something before they read a word. Motion as
narrative rather than decoration.

The gap between competent and exceptional here is not technique — it is having
an aesthetic position and holding it across projects. Technique is purchasable;
a point of view is not.

**Instrument:** the taste engine. It currently has almost no data, which makes
this the domain most bottlenecked on evidence rather than ability.
